How it is calculated

  • EOQ = √(2 × annual units × ordering cost / holding cost per unit). Safety stock = z × daily demand std dev × √lead time (days). ROP = demand during lead time + safety stock. Max = ROP + EOQ.
  • ABC uses cumulative value before each SKU; XYZ uses the coefficient of variation (CV) of monthly demand.
  • Limits: simulated data, simulation uses the same 12 months as the analysis (in-sample), demand assumed normal, stockouts are lost sales (no backorders).
  • On real data, replace ordering cost, holding cost and lead times with the company's own figures.
